# A model over all the tested strategies' trades: is there an edge in their combination? (registered before any outcome)

**Registered 2026-11-14, before any feature, model or outcome below was computed.** Fadi: the
price alone shows no edge, but the trades of the strategies tested so far, many of them near zero
before costs, might hold one in combination ("tens of trades a day"); a model should look for it.
Claude laid out what was tried before and the risks; Fadi: "yes".

## 1. What was tried before (disclosed)

- P4 meta-labeling (LR, LightGBM, MLP) on the L1 SMC setups: no skill (Brier skill < 0).
- AHF filters and meta-labeling on the AH clone; a model of the five's losers: no skill.
- **Confluence** (`docs/portfolio/CONFLUENCE.md`): a combination of strategies, strong on gold
  in-sample, failed on six other markets. A model can find exactly this kind of pattern.
- Every strategy in the pool below was already measured on 2012 to 2026-01-09 one by one (about
  100 registered looks). **The test years are therefore not fresh for the strategies; they are fresh
  only for the combinations the model learns.** The only data no research has used is FxPro GOLD
  2026-01-12 to 2026-11-05 (about ten months), as before.

## 2. The candidate pool (XAUUSD 5m, research store, 2012 to 2026-01-09)

Every trade any of these makes, with its own entry, stop, target and exit (no rule changed):

1. **The lab strategies** (`gold_lab.all_signals`, both bundles M0 and M8; stored in
   `gold_lab_trades.parquet`): S1, S4, S6, S6R, S7, NYMOM, NEWS830, EXTREME, EMAX, EMAX_R, RSI2,
   LVL4H, PIVOT, GAP, USDLEAD, SPREAD, BIGDAY and the rest of its 22 names.
2. **The level families** (`app/levels`, 24 families x break / rejection, `SPEC.md`), as
   `levels discover` makes them (the real families only, no shifted copies): 2012-2021 and
   2024-2026 from the stored runs, 2022-2023 generated by the same code.
3. **The six level strategies** (`gold_live.five_trades` with ROUND).

A **cell** is one strategy x bundle (lab), family x behaviour (levels) or one of the six.

**Selection, on the training years only:** a cell enters if, on 2012-2019, it has at least 200
trades and a **gross** mean R of at least −0.02 (about break-even or better before costs: Fadi's
"slight or zero edge before costs"). Nothing after 2019 decides membership.

## 3. The unit, the label, the features (all known at the entry)

Unit: one candidate trade (cell, entry bar, direction). Label: its **net R** after its own costs
and stop slip.

Features (only bars that closed before the entry bar, and trades that closed before it):

- **who:** the cell (categorical), its family group (lab / levels / six), the direction;
- **when:** New York minute of the day, weekday;
- **how big the day is:** part 1's out-of-sample volatility forecast (log F, `VOL_FORECAST.md`), its
  ratio to the ATR model, and u (the range used so far over the forecast, `EXHAUSTION.md`); ATR(1h) /
  ATR(4h), ATR(4h) / ATR(D1); the cost in R;
- **the others (the combination):** in the 60 minutes before the entry, the number of candidate
  entries of each group in the same direction and in the opposite direction; the number of the six
  open in the same and in the opposite direction (k); the number of cells that fired that day so far;
- **recent results:** the mean net R of the cell's last 20 closed trades, and of all candidates
  closed in the previous 5 trading days.

## 4. The models (fixed here; nothing tuned on the test)

- **Ridge** (standardised features, the cell one-hot, alpha 10): the linear baseline.
- **LightGBM** regressor on net R: num_leaves 15, n_estimators 400, learning_rate 0.03,
  min_child_samples 200, subsample 0.8 (every iteration), colsample_bytree 0.8, seed 20261120,
  no early stopping.

**Walk-forward, yearly, expanding:** each year from 2020 on is predicted by models fitted on every
earlier year from 2012, with a **purge**: training trades whose exit is on or after the first day
of the predicted year are dropped, and a 1-day embargo.

## 5. The trading rule

Take a candidate when its predicted net R > tau. **tau** is chosen once, on **2020-2021 (the
validation years)**, from {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15}: the value with the highest mean net R per
selected trade among those that select at least one trade a day on average; ties go to the lower
value. The model (Ridge or LightGBM) is chosen the same way, on 2020-2021 only. Both are then fixed
for the test.

Taken trades are equal-risk; several may be open at once. A day's result is the sum of net R of the
trades taken that day.

## 6. The test (2022-01-01 to 2026-01-09) and the pass rule

The chosen model and tau, walk-forward as §4, on the test years. **It passes** if all of:

1. **Edge:** mean net R per selected trade > 0, one-sided p < 0.05 by a 4-week block bootstrap of
   days (10,000 resamples, seed 20261121).
2. **Better than chance selection:** the selected trades' mean net R is above the 95th percentile of
   200 random selections that take, each day, the same number of candidates at random from that
   day's pool (seed 20261122).
3. **Useful:** the daily results' correlation with the five's daily results is below +0.2, or the
   five plus the selection has a higher daily Sharpe than the five alone.
4. **Unseen data:** on FxPro GOLD 2026-01-12 to 2026-11-05 (every cell regenerated on that feed,
   summary bars dropped; the model fitted on 2012 to 2026-01-09 of the store, tau as chosen), the mean
   net R per selected trade is > 0. About ten months: a check, not proof.

If it passes, a separate registration decides any live use (the EA or the service). If it fails, the
search for an edge in gold's intraday trades ends with it.

## 7. Reported with it (decide nothing)

- The pool: cells kept and dropped by §2, candidates a day, per year.
- Validation (2020-2021): every model x tau.
- Test per year; selected trades a day; by cell group; the largest drawdown in R.
- LightGBM's gain by feature: which features carried the selection.
- **Effective sample:** the number of distinct trading days; trades on one day are not independent,
  so every interval is by day blocks, never by trade.

## Addendum 1 (2026-11-14, while writing the code, before any build or run)

1. **The level families are regenerated for every year** (2012 to 2026-01-09) by today's
   `app/levels` code in one run (real families only), instead of joining the stored runs with
   2022-2023: one code version for all years. The build compares its 2012-2021 trades with the
   stored run (count and net sum) and reports it.
2. **The volatility features are out of sample from 2013:** each year's log F and M3 / ATR ratio
   come from M3 and M1 fitted on the earlier years only (yearly, expanding); 2012 has none (missing,
   which LightGBM takes as such and Ridge fills with the training median plus a missing flag). A
   candidate entering before 02:00 New York has none either: the day's forecast is issued at 02:00.
3. **Every feature of a candidate entering at bar e reads bars up to e - 1** (also for the lab
   strategies that enter at a bar's close: one bar of information given up, never one too many),
   and only trades whose exit bar is before e. A test changes every bar from e on and every
   outcome of trades closing at or after e, and the features do not move.
4. **FxPro 2026:** the dollar's-lead lab cell (USDLEAD) cannot be made there (its driver feeds are
   sealed after 2026-01-09); if kept, it is absent from the FxPro readout only.
