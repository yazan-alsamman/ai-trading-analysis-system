## G1 duplicates

Normalised text: lower case, every run of non-letters (punctuation, digits, symbols) becomes a space, spaces collapsed. Rows are taken in date order (file order on ties); the earliest is kept. Done before the split. Two sets (decided 2026-11-19):

- **Classifier set** (train / validation / test of the classifier): exact duplicates of the normalised text dropped at any distance in time (so a recurring price-report template cannot sit in two splits); then a headline whose token set has Jaccard >= 0.8 with a kept headline dated up to 3 days earlier (same day included) is dropped.
- **Index set** (the daily index, PREREG §5): exact and near duplicates dropped only when a kept headline is dated up to 3 days earlier (the same story on several sites); a template headline on another day is that day's news and stays.
