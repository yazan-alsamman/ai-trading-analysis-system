**What is already checked by code:** the five written conditions (level, sweep, CHoCH, FVG and plan)

**The one question for each chart:** would an SMC trader recognise this as a genuine liquidity
sweep, a change of character and a fair value gap, as defined in the spec's "Written pattern
definition"?

- **Sweep:** price trades beyond the level and closes back.
- **CHoCH:** a bar *closes* beyond the reference structure level; a wick is not enough.
- **FVG:** a three-bar gap of at least the minimum size.
- **Timing:** the gap may form on the CHoCH bar or up to 6 bars before it (the panel prints the
  offset). A gap before the CHoCH is not, by itself, a reason for ❌. The setup is confirmed at
  the CHoCH bar's close, when every condition is known.

- **✅ valid pattern**
- **❌ not a genuine pattern.** Name what you see on the chart, for example "the sweep is noise
  inside a range" or "the CHoCH breaks a trivial micro-swing".
- **⚠️ unsure**

Use the last column for feedback on the definition itself, for example "minimum sweep depth too
small". Definition feedback is decided once, after the review. The gate is **45 or more ✅ of
50**.
