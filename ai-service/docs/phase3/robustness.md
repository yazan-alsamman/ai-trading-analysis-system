## Leakage

- Every training row closed at least a day before its test window opened: **yes** (7 windows).
- The EV decision uses the model's probability, the planned R and the round-trip cost in R. All three are fixed at the CHoCH close, before the fill and the outcome.

## Costs

- Net R equals realized R minus the round-trip cost for every row: **yes**. The cost definition is `CostParams` defaults throughout (spread plus the larger of 50% or $0.10; $0.10 slippage on stops, inside realized R). The 2× stress doubles the round-trip cost, not the stop slippage.
