## 5. `libs/` report (inspection only, for Fadi and Yazan)

| Check | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Ambiguous bar (stop and target in one bar) | ✅ counts as a stop (`libs/backtest/trading_backtest/engine.py:294`, stop first) |
| Costs | ❌ fees and slippage are rates (bps of price). v0.2 needs the bar's recorded spread + max(50 %, $0.10), slippage on stop fills, and a 2× cost run. Not supported today |
| Position size for JPY pairs | ❌ `Sizer.size` (`libs/backtest/trading_backtest/config.py`) treats the balance as quote currency, with no quote → account conversion: USD/JPY comes out about 150× too small. USD-quoted XAU/USD and EUR/USD are unaffected for a USD account |
| No look-ahead in indicators | ✅ every indicator has an append-future-bars test and a change-a-future-bar test, bit-for-bit (`libs/indicators/tests/test_no_lookahead.py`). ⚠️ They run on continuous hourly synthetic bars, so they do not cover FX weekend and daily-break gaps |
