## 1. The clock of four pairs changes over time (blocks P8 for those pairs)

The importer converts every file with `Europe/Athens`. For EURUSD and XAUUSD the evidence agrees
in every year: the week opens at 17:00 (EURUSD) or 18:00 (XAUUSD) New York, and the jobs-report
spike (08:30 New York) lands at 08:30, also in the weeks when US and EU daylight saving disagree.

For GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD and USDCAD it does not:

| Years | Jobs-report peak | Week open / close (NY) | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| first year → 2021 | mostly 09:30 | Sun 18:00 / Fri 15:54 | timestamps 1 h later than `Europe/Athens` |
| 2022 | split 07:30 / 08:30 | Sun 16:03 / Fri 15:54 | a transition inside the year |
| 2023 → 2026-01 | 08:30 | Sun 17:00 / Fri 16:54-16:59 | consistent with `Europe/Athens` |

So, before 2023, the history of these four pairs was stored on a different clock than the current
server. Converting it with `Europe/Athens` puts every bar about one hour off. That would break
sessions, news timing and the multi-timeframe alignment. These pairs belong to P8. The options
(open):

- detect each period's offset from the evidence (week open and jobs-report peak) and correct it in
  the importer, with the offsets and their evidence written to the report;
- use these four pairs from 2023 only;
- take the older years from another source (Dukascopy) after the P1 cross-check.

XAUUSD (v1) and EURUSD are not affected.

## 2. Other findings

- **XAUUSD spread.** 84% of 2010 bars have spread 0 (missing in practice). The median is
  $0.41-0.70 in 2011-2015, falls to $0.05-0.08 in 2021-2023 and is $0.15-0.16 in 2024-2025. The cost
  model adds max(50%, $0.10) to the recorded spread, so the $0.10 floor dominates recent years.
- **EURUSD spread.** 0 on 63-70% of bars in 2021-2023. The recorded spread cannot be the only cost
  input for EURUSD in those years.
- **XAUUSD early years.** The jobs-report check is weak in 2010-2013 (the peak is rarely exactly at
  08:30). These years are outside or at the start of the development period (2012-2017); to look at
  before tuning.
- **Bars in closed hours.** XAUUSD has 939 bars inside the 17:00-18:00 New York daily break (it
  closes later on some days). The four pairs have bars on Sunday 16:00 and Friday 17:00 New York:
  the clock shift above.
- **Longest holes.** 2025-07-02 20:03 New York (about 9 h, XAUUSD and EURUSD) and 2024-07-02 05:09
  New York (about 12 h, EURUSD). These should be checked against Dukascopy in P1 task 5.
