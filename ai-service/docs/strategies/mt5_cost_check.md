**Correction (2026-11-05): the replay was not on real ticks.** For every instrument the tester log
says "2024.01.01 00:00 - 2024.07.02 00:00 no real ticks, every tick generation used". The FxPro
server keeps real ticks only from about 2026-01, so MT5 generated the ticks from M1 bars. The
tester cost below is the M1 bar's spread plus FxPro's commission, not the spread at the fill.

Costs in R a trade. The modelled cost is the registered round trip, plus the SL slippage on SL exits. The tester cost is the research gross R minus the tester's net R before swap, on the trades whose exit kind agrees (both TP, both SL, or both at the time). The tester fills stops at their level, so it measures spread and commission, not slippage.
